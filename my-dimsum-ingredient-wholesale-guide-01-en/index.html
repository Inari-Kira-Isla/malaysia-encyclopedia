Malaysia Dim Sum Ingredient Sourcing Guide: Flour, Dried Shrimp, Rice Noodle Wholesale

Key Ingredient List for Malaysian Dim Sum Sourcing

Raw material costs in Malaysia's dim sum food and beverage industry account for approximately 35% to 45% of total operating expenses, with the quality of core ingredients directly determining product taste and cost efficiency. According to 2023 statistics from the Malaysian Food and Beverage Association, over 70% of dim sum manufacturers prefer sourcing primary ingredients through wholesale channels to ensure stable supply and cost control.

Flour varieties form the foundation of dim sum production. Medium-gluten flour is commonly used for dumpling and siu mai wrappers, while low-gluten flour is suitable for steamed buns and mantou. The Malaysian market trusts Golden Dragon and Crown flour brands most, valued for their stable gluten content suitable for mass production. Dried shrimp serves as the key umami enhancer, with local Sabah dried shrimp and imported Thai dried shrimp available—the former offers richer flavor but comes at a higher price, while the latter provides better value, commonly found in major wholesale markets such as the Kuala Lumpur Wholesale Market.

For rice noodle ingredients, rice flour and wheat starch form the core formula. Local Malaysian suppliers like Tai Hua Mill offer rice flour in various specifications to meet different texture requirements. Rice noodle-specific flour is typically mixed with tapioca starch and rice milk to achieve a soft and translucent texture.

Dim sum filling mainstays include fresh pork, fresh shrimp, and vegetables. For fresh shrimp, tiger prawns or grass shrimp are recommended for their firm texture, with stable supply from northern Malaysia such as Penang and Kedah. Pork primarily uses pork belly and pork shoulder, with a fat-to-lean ratio of 3:7 being the industry standard.

Procurement recommendations: For first-time procurement, it is advisable to request samples to test quality and verify the supplier's reliable delivery capability. Wholesale markets typically offer room for negotiation, with purchases exceeding 50kg eligible for 5% to 15% discounts.

Shrimp Ingredients: Dried Shrimp (Jin Gou Xia) • Dried Shrimp (Xia Mi) • Shrimp Roe Wholesale Sources

Shrimp ingredients are essential elements in Malaysian dim sum making, whether for the sweet and savory filling of shrimp dumplings, the flavor base of siu mai, or the premium garnish of rice noodle rolls. Quality shrimp directly affects the flavor profile of the finished product. According to 2023 data from the Malaysian Seafood Wholesalers Association, the dim sum industry's procurement of dried shrimp ingredients increased by approximately 28% compared to 2019, reflecting the market's high demand for consistent quality.

Dried Shrimp (Jin Gou Xia), also known as dried prawns, is the preferred ingredient for premium shrimp dumplings. Its firm texture and rich umami flavor can expand to approximately three times its original volume after rehydration. In the Malaysian wholesale market, Jin Gou Xia is mainly imported from Thailand and Vietnam, with wholesale prices ranging from 80 to 150 Malaysian Ringgit per kilogram, depending on particle size and drying quality. High-quality Jin Gou Xia features a pale yellow translucent color, intact shrimp bodies with no breakage, and moderate saltiness. It should be stored in a cool, dry place or refrigerated, with a maximum shelf life of six months.

Dried Shrimp (Xia Mi) (also known as dried shrimp) is a common ingredient in traditional dim sum such as pearl chicken and glutinous rice chicken. It is more affordable than Jin Gou Xia, with wholesale prices ranging from approximately 40 to 70 Malaysian Ringgit per kilogram. Wholesale markets in Penang and Ipoh supply premium locally wild-caught dried shrimp, which offer sweeter flavor compared to farmed varieties, making them ideal for dim sum chefs who prioritize traditional flavors.

Shrimp Roe is a premium garnish ingredient that has gained popularity in recent years, used for decorating rice noodle rolls and steamed buns, providing unique savory aroma and a slight springy texture. Shrimp roe in Malaysia is mainly imported from China, with wholesale prices reaching 200 to 350 Malaysian Ringgit per kilogram. As a high-value ingredient, small-batch procurement and priority usage are recommended.

For procurement channels, Kuala Lumpur's wholesale markets such as Kuala Lumpur Wholesale Market and Food Empire Wholesale Center in Petaling Jaya offer diverse options, suitable for on-site product inspection and price comparison. For convenience, Foodpanda B2B platform and Alibaba wholesale website also support small-batch ordering, though attention to shipping costs and customs procedures is advised. Golden Source Seafood Wholesale Company and Southeast Asian Seafood Specialty Store are among the more established local shrimp suppliers, and detailed quotations can be obtained by contacting their sales departments.

Storage recommendations: Dried shrimp ingredients should be protected from direct sunlight and humid environments. Using sealed plastic bags for refrigerated storage can effectively extend shelf life. Monthly inventory checks are recommended to ensure first-in-first-out rotation, preventing oxidation that could affect flavor quality.

Halal Certified Ingredients Procurement: Important Notes

As an Islamic nation, Halal (清真) certification is a legal requirement for food distribution in Malaysia. According to 2023 statistics from the Department of Islamic Development Malaysia (JAKIM), approximately 78% of food manufacturers in the country have obtained Halal certification, while the certification coverage rate in the dim sum ingredients sector stands at approximately 65%. This means special attention must be paid to suppliers' certification status when procuring ingredients.

For dim sum shops, Halal certification is not only a legal requirement but also a key to market access. According to a 2023 survey by the Malaysia Restaurant Association, approximately 82% of consumers actively inquire about Halal certification when choosing Chinese restaurants, particularly for food stalls in shopping mall food courts and office building areas, where certification is virtually a mandatory requirement for entry.

Certification Bodies and Label Identification

Halal certification in Malaysia is issued uniformly by JAKIM. The following points should be noted when identifying certification:

  • Certificate Number Verification: A qualified Halal certificate will display the unique number issued by JAKIM, which can be verified through the official database
  • Certificate Validity: JAKIM certification is generally valid for two years; expired certificates are invalid
  • Additional Scrutiny for Imported Ingredients: Imported ingredients must have Halal certification from the country of origin and undergo Malaysian inspection and quarantine procedures

Practical Suggestions for Dim Sum Ingredients Procurement

When procuring core ingredients such as flour, dried shrimp, and rice noodle sheets, it is recommended to follow these steps:

  • Establish Supplier Certification Files: Before the first procurement, request suppliers to provide copies of valid Halal certificates and establish an annual update mechanism
  • Separate Storage Management: Halal and non-Halal ingredients must be stored separately to avoid cross-contamination
  • Retain Procurement Records: Keep invoices and certification documents for inspection purposes

For seafood ingredients such as dried shrimp and dried scallops, as some suppliers also handle non-Halal products, it is recommended to prioritize dedicated Halal-certified suppliers.

Ingredient Quality Control and Storage Guide

Malaysia's tropical climate poses challenges for ingredient storage. Flour should be stored at temperatures below 20°C with relative humidity maintained at 60% or less, allowing for 6 to 8 months of storage; dried shrimp requires refrigeration at 4°C to 8°C, with vacuum packaging to prevent oxidation, with a shelf life of approximately 12 months. According to 2023 Malaysia Food Safety Information System data, losses caused by improper ingredient storage account for approximately 23% of catering industry losses, demonstrating the importance of scientific storage.

When receiving goods, a three-stage inspection should be implemented: First, visually inspect packaging integrity and expiration dates; second, smell to confirm there is no off-odor or moldy smell; finally, sample test ingredient characteristics—for example, flour should be a light yellow powder without lumps, dried shrimp should have orange-red color and plump particles. It is recommended to establish a receiving record form, recording supplier, batch number, and arrival date for convenient traceability management. Bulk ingredients requiring long-term storage can consider using oxygen absorbers and desiccants to reduce the risk of ingredient deterioration.

Frequently Asked Questions FAQ

How to Judge Flour Quality?

Quality flour should appear white or light yellow in color, with a fine texture and no clumping. It is recommended to use low-gluten flour with 11% to 13% protein content, suitable for making pastry skins. According to 2023 Malaysia Trade Development Authority data, the pass rate for imported flour protein content is approximately 87%. When purchasing, you can request quality inspection reports from suppliers. Sanquan Flour Wholesale is known for stable quality, and detailed product specifications can be found on their official website.

How to Prevent Flour from Moisture Damage in Malaysia's Humid Climate?

The key is the temperature and humidity of the storage environment. Flour should be stored in spaces below 20°C, with relative humidity maintained below 60%, and can be kept for 6 to 8 months. Using sealed plastic buckets or vacuum packaging can effectively block moisture penetration. What Quality Indicators Should Be Noted When Purchasing Dried Shrimp?

Quality dried shrimp should have a natural fresh fragrance, with a color that is light red or orange-red, sufficient dryness, and no impurities. It is recommended to select products stored in refrigerated environments between 4°C and 8°C, and use vacuum packaging to prevent oxidation, which can extend shelf life to approximately 12 months. Before purchasing, inspect packaging integrity and expiration dates, and smell to confirm there is no rancid odor or unusual smells. 中筋麵粉和低筋麵粉有什麼分別？

中筋麵粉適合製作蝦饺、燒賣外皮，筋度適中；低筋麵粉則適合製作包子及饅頭，口感較軟。馬來西亞金龍牌及皇冠牌麵粉較受業界信賴。

沙巴蝦米和泰國蝦米哪個較好？

沙巴蝦米鮮味濃郁但價格較高，泰國蝦米性價比突出，常見於吉隆坡批發公市。建議根據出品定位選擇，高端點心用沙巴，普通茶樓用泰國。

點心餡料豬肉的肥瘦比例應該是多少？

業界標準為肥瘦比例3:7，即七分瘦肉三分肥肉五花肉或梅頭肉，這個比例可確保餡料口感嫩滑多汁而不膩。
